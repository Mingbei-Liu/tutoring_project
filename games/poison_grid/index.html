Spatial strategy

Poison Grid

Players alternate choosing one remaining tile. The chosen tile and every remaining tile above it and to its right are removed. The bottom-left tile is poisoned: whoever takes it loses.

Think in shapes

Every move changes the boundary of the board.

The remaining tiles always form a staircase-like region anchored at the poisoned bottom-left corner. Strong play requires anticipating how your move changes the safe choices available to the other player.
