Arithmetic challenge

The 24 Game

Use each of the four numbers exactly once to make 24. You may use addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and parentheses.

Your four numbers

Can you make 24?

Solvable puzzle
3
3
8
8
Use every number once Target: 24 Allowed: + − × ÷ ( )

One possible solution

8 ÷ (3 − 8 ÷ 3) = 24

Other valid solutions may also exist.

Why this helps

Build flexible mathematical thinking.

The 24 Game encourages students to test multiple strategies, work backward from a target, recognize useful number relationships, and become more comfortable with arithmetic operations.
