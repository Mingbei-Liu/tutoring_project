Strategy and computer behavior

Every computer move follows the same rules as yours.

The optimal opponent evaluates the complete two-pile game and chooses a move that forces a win whenever one exists. The casual opponent chooses a gentle random move 90% of the time, favoring smaller removals so the game lasts longer, and uses the optimal strategy 10% of the time.
